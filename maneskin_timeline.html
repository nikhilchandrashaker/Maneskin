MÅNESKIN

Four schoolmates busking on a Roman side street became the band that broke Italy's 31-year Eurovision drought — then took glam rock to arenas that hadn't booked a rock act in years. This is how it happened.

2015–2016
▸Four schoolmates, one Facebook ad
Damiano David, Victoria De Angelis and Thomas Raggi knew each other from school in Rome. Needing a drummer, they posted a "musicians wanted" ad on Facebook — Ethan Torchio answered. The four began busking on Via del Corso and playing small venues before entering a local youth competition called "Pulse," writing original songs for the first time to qualify.
2016
▸A name born from moonlight
Entering "Pulse" meant they needed a band name. Victoria, who is half-Danish, suggested Måneskin — Danish for "moonlight." It stuck.
2017
▸Runner-up on X Factor Italia
The band auditioned for the eleventh season of X Factor Italia with their original song "Chosen," mentored by Manuel Agnelli. They finished second overall, but a busking-style cover they performed on the show — a stripped-down version of "Beggin'" — quietly became their calling card long before anyone knew it would go viral years later.
Debut single "Chosen" — #2, Italian singles chart
Dec 2017 – Oct 2018
▸From EP to debut album
The "Chosen" EP arrived in December 2017. Ten months later, on 26 October 2018, they released their debut full-length album, Il ballo della vita ("The Dance of Life"), establishing the glam-rock, funk-inflected sound and stage persona that would define them.
March 2021
▸Sanremo, then a second album
Måneskin won the 71st Sanremo Music Festival — Italy's biggest and most prestigious song contest — with "Zitti e buoni," earning the automatic right to represent Italy at Eurovision. Days later they released their second studio album, Teatro d'ira: Vol. I ("Theatre of Wrath, Vol. I").
22 May 2021
▸Eurovision, Rotterdam
Performing "Zitti e buoni," Måneskin won the Eurovision Song Contest 2021 with 524 points — Italy's first victory since 1990. The win instantly turned a well-known Italian rock band into a global act, with hosts and press reduced to spelling the umlaut in real time.
2021
▸The viral aftershock
In the months after Eurovision, that old X Factor cover of "Beggin'" exploded on TikTok and streaming platforms worldwide, joined by "I Wanna Be Your Slave." Måneskin became the first Italian rock act to reach the UK Top 10, with all three songs charting internationally — over three million copies sold outside Italy alone.
Nov 2022
▸A Grammy nomination
Måneskin received their first Grammy nomination, for Best New Artist at the 65th Annual Grammy Awards — a rare nod for a band singing largely in Italian.
Jan 2023
▸RUSH!
Their third studio album, RUSH!, arrived on 20 January 2023 — largely written and sung in English, aimed squarely at the arena and stadium circuit they'd earned across Europe and the Americas.
2024
▸Hiatus, and a solo turn
Frontman Damiano David launched a solo career in 2024, and the band has been on an indefinite hiatus since. No breakup was announced — just a pause, after an eight-year run from a Roman side street to a Eurovision trophy and a Grammy nomination.

Numbers behind the noise

Three ways to see the same story: a band with a solid home following in Italy that, almost overnight in May 2021, became one on every chart and every streaming service in the world.

Fan support before and after Eurovision

Måneskin had 2.4 million monthly Spotify listeners the week of the Eurovision final. Five weeks later they'd passed 50 million — briefly outstreaming Foo Fighters and Arctic Monkeys combined.

How far each album travelled

Their 2018 debut never charted outside Italy. By Rush! in 2023, they were Top 5 in the UK and had debuted on the Billboard 200 in the US — their first entry on an American chart.

The night the televote decided it

Italy sat outside the top three after national juries voted — 4th place. The public televote, worth half the total score, gave them 316 of their 524 points and the win.
